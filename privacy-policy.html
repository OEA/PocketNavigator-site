Pocket Navigator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your location is used only to show where you are inside the venue and to guide you there.
  • Location is used in the background only during a route you started, and stops when you arrive or end the route.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who we are

Pocket Navigator (“the app”) is published by Omer Emre Aslan (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data processed through the app. You can contact us at oeaslandev@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app gives indoor turn-by-turn directions inside supported venues (currently Boston Logan International Airport, Terminal E). It uses Bluetooth beacons installed in the venue to work out your indoor position, and can keep guiding you on the Lock Screen while your phone is locked.

Information we use

InformationWhyWhere it is processed
Precise location (indoor position from Bluetooth beacons and iOS Location Services) To show where you are in the venue, calculate a route and guide you to your destination On your device, and by our positioning provider Pointr (see below)

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other content from your device. The places you search for and the routes you take are not tied to your identity.

Location and background use

  • The app asks for location access “While Using the App” only. It never asks for “Always”.
  • When you start a route and lock your phone, guidance continues in the background so the Lock Screen stays up to date. iOS shows a location indicator while this happens.
  • Background location stops automatically when you arrive, when you tap End, or when you turn off Pocket mode in Settings.
  • Bluetooth is used only to detect the venue's positioning beacons.
  • You can change or withdraw location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Pocket Navigator. Without it, the app can show the venue and its places but can't guide you.

Notifications and Live Activities

Step alerts (before lifts and stairs) and the arrival alert are created on your device; we don't use a push notification server. The Lock Screen Live Activity is also updated on your device. You can turn both off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Service providers

Indoor positioning, venue maps and routing are provided by Pointr Ltd, which processes the information described above on our behalf and only to provide this service. See Pointr's privacy policy.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking services, and we don't sell or share personal data for advertising.
